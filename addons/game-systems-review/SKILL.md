# Game systems review

Read [native execution and inputs](NATIVE-BINDINGS.md) and [game evidence qualifications](references/method-notes.md) first. A game design document (GDD) describes a game's rules and intended experience; a design pillar names an experience the game should support.

Choose the complete method the request needs:

- [Numeric balance review](references/balance-check.md): combat, economy, progression or loot data compared with actual design targets; distinguish measured outliers from missing targets.
- [Cross-GDD review](references/cross-gdd-review.md): consistency, design theory or both, followed by interaction scenarios and an evidence-backed report. Read each linked phase when it is reached, including its complete checklist. Report documents reviewed out of documents present, every unread/stub document, and every phase not assessed.
- [Game designer expertise](references/game-designer.md): relevant domain reasoning when the review needs it; this is expertise, not another installed agent.

Preserve the user's model, effort, scope and project conventions. These methods provide game design review, not a full studio, prototype builder, engine runtime, fixed review team or automatic project stage. Ordinary artifact authority permits the requested report; findings do not authorize unrelated changes, commits or publication. An automated or document review cannot establish fun, difficulty or player experience without the corresponding evidence.

Original Donchitos methods, MIT notice, exact hashes and replayable adaptation diffs are recorded in [SOURCE.json](SOURCE.json). Retained files under `upstream/` are provenance only; follow active references and native callbacks.

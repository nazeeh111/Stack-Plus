# Interpreting game-design evidence

The retained methods contain useful game-design questions and illustrative theories. Their original wording is preserved under `upstream/`, and the adaptation diffs show the native qualifications. Do not turn a framework, example or plausible preference into a measured law.

- Ask whether the intended game needs a primary loop or intentionally supports several. Compare the actual brief, goals and player observations before calling competing loops a defect.
- Count simultaneous decisions to identify a usability hypothesis. The source's three-to-four attention heuristic is not a universal cognitive limit. Timing, familiarity, interface and player population matter; seek an actual scenario or playtest.
- A ranged option dealing 80% of melee damage is not by itself a mathematically dominant strategy. Compare feasible states, accuracy, range, risk, resource costs, opportunity costs and counters. Distinguish strict dominance from a promising strategy to investigate.
- Claimed incompatible fantasies or pillar drift need the actual declared pillars and the scenario in which they conflict. Deliberate contrast can serve the design. Do not invent pillars from genre expectations.
- The game-designer reference's 30-second loops, 5–15-minute sessions, 0.5-second feedback and sawtooth difficulty are source design examples, not required timings for every game. Motivation taxonomies and MDA (mechanics, dynamics and aesthetics) organize questions; they do not establish that a design is fun.
- Sources and sinks are how resources enter and leave a game economy. An unbounded source or positive feedback loop can be calculated from actual rules, but whether the resulting surplus is undesirable depends on the intended experience. Name missing rates, targets, caps, session length and units.
- Source recommendations for Gini coefficients, reward guarantees, external tuning files and document tiers apply only where the game's goals and actual review contract make them relevant. They are not newly installed requirements or authority to change monetization, architecture or project conventions.

For numeric results, show formulas, units, source values and assumptions so another reader can reproduce them. Missing targets and missing play evidence remain explicit gaps. Document review and toy fixtures do not prove native automatic skill selection, engine execution, balance quality or human enjoyment.

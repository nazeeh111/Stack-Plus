# AI Scientist study pipeline

This authored native entry links the original computational-research methods.
Read [NATIVE-BINDINGS.md](NATIVE-BINDINGS.md), the installed **AI Scientist v3**
runtime guide and the complete [pipeline method](methods/docs/pipeline.md).
The runtime's actual phase acceptance determines readiness; transport or an
installed Python environment alone does not establish a completed study.

Use the original six-stage orchestrator and its shared run directory. Read the
relevant complete phase methods rather than replacing their contracts with a
summary:

- [Draft/qualify the task](methods/src/agents/skills/draft-task/METHOD.md).
- [Form an experiment idea](methods/src/agents/skills/create-idea/METHOD.md),
  [create the next program](methods/src/agents/skills/edit-program/METHOD.md),
  [evaluate it](methods/src/agents/skills/evaluate-program/METHOD.md) and
  [verify its rubrics](methods/src/agents/skills/verify-rubrics/METHOD.md).
- Keep [run-local memory](methods/src/agents/skills/memory-log/METHOD.md) and
  [scientific evidence](methods/src/agents/skills/scientific-evidence/METHOD.md).
- [Retrieve references](methods/src/agents/skills/retrieve-references/METHOD.md),
  [write the paper](methods/src/agents/skills/write-paper/METHOD.md) or its
  [standalone narrative](methods/src/agents/skills/write-paper-standalone/METHOD.md),
  [validate artifacts/compile](methods/src/agents/skills/validate-paper/METHOD.md)
  and [review the draft](methods/src/agents/skills/review-paper/METHOD.md).
  [Concept figures](methods/src/agents/skills/generate-concept-figure/METHOD.md)
  apply when the actual paper strategy requests that workflow.

The pipeline guide owns the baselines/ablations, sealed final evaluation, post-hoc
stages, resume commands and publication eligibility. Its task-qualified path is
distinct from a legacy example or reduced smoke run. Preserve search/final data
separation and failed/missing episodes, then validate manuscript claims against
actual retained artifacts. A generated paper or a high search score alone does
not establish scientific validity or research novelty.
